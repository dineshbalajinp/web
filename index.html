About

I'm Dinesh — a web and mobile developer based in Austin, working with founders and small teams who need one person to own the whole build, design through deployment.

Six years in, split fairly evenly between client-facing web products and mobile apps for iOS and Android. I care most about the parts users never notice: load times, tap targets that are actually easy to tap, and forms that don't lose your data.

Outside of client work, I maintain a couple of open-source React Native libraries and write occasionally about performance budgets.
