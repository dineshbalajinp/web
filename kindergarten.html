About the tool

Running a kindergarten means juggling attendance, daily updates, fees and constant parent questions. This system puts it all in one place.

The problem. Paper registers, WhatsApp groups and scattered spreadsheets make it hard for schools to keep records accurate and for parents to know what their child did today.

The solution. A single system with three connected parts: administrators manage the school on the web, teachers record the day from their phones, and parents see updates the moment they happen.

Web and mobile, in sync. Everything a teacher enters in the app appears in the admin portal and in the parent app — no double entry, no lost notes.

Teacher app

Fast, tap-friendly tools for the classroom so paperwork never gets in the way of teaching.
